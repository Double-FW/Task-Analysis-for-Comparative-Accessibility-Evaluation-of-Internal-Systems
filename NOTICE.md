# Attribution and source boundaries

Original repository guidance is authored by Gareth Ford Williams for Double FW Ltd and distributed under [MIT](LICENSE), copyright 2026 Double FW Ltd. MIT is the sole project licence. Retain the copyright and permission notice with copies or substantial portions.

Linked standards, methodologies, tools and other third-party works retain their own rights. Linking to them or describing a method does not copy their contents, establish ownership or relicense them under MIT. Verify actual rights and conditions before importing external text, assets, data or code into a public training export.

The maintained method and agent safeguards use version 1.2.0. Earlier source snapshots and duplicated embedded RAG bodies have been replaced by maintained navigation; historical versions remain in Git history. Do not ingest historical and current copies as independent current authorities.

The record template, fictional arithmetic and twenty example prompts are illustrative aids, not participant evidence, executed tests, validated scores, organisational approval or legal findings. No actual employee or confidential study data is included or authorised for publication by these documents. Keep source provenance and actual evidence/decision dates separate from editorial and export dates.
